A small privacy notice

To enter OKAY, BUT WHY?, you can choose whether to allow a limited visitor notice to be sent to the site owner. This is designed to help the owner understand basic device/browser access and is not intended to identify you personally.

If you choose “I Agree,” this site sends only:

  • Device type and device/model information when the browser makes it available
  • Browser, operating platform, and platform version when available
  • Screen size, language, visit time, and the page URL being visited

This visitor notice does not intentionally collect your name, email address, precise location, passwords, camera, microphone, or the contents of what you read.

The site owner intends to keep this information limited and confidential and use it only for the stated website-visit purpose. No information is sent by this visitor notice if you choose “I Disagree.”

The visitor notice is delivered through FormSubmit, a third-party form relay. Because the request passes through that service, its own privacy practices may also apply. FormSubmit states that user-submitted form information is processed to provide its service and that submissions are retained for 30 days. See its privacy information below.

You chose not to allow the limited visitor notice, so the site remains locked. You can change your choice by selecting “Review privacy choice.”

Privacy information is based on the Philippine National Privacy Commission's guidance on transparency, consent, proportionality, and the right to be informed. NPC: Right to Be Informed · NPC: Data Privacy Principles · FormSubmit Privacy
